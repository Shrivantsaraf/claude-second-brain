# n8n

**One-liner:** Open-source, self-hostable, **visual** agent/workflow builder (1,800+ nodes) —
drag-and-drop automations. your top "try next" tool.

## What it is
A workflow-automation platform where you wire up nodes (triggers, apps, logic, AI) on a canvas
instead of writing all the glue code. Self-hostable for free. Big, active ecosystem.

- **Free** to self-host → fits the zero-cost rule.
- **Visual** → great for learning agentic concepts and for *showing* builds in brand content.
- **Huge freelance market** → clients love n8n automations, so skills here convert to money.
