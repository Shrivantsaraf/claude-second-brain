# Agentic AI

**One-liner:** AI that doesn't just chat — it **plans and takes actions** through tools (calls
APIs, edits files, runs steps) to accomplish a goal. your #1 learning goal.

## What it is
The shift from "LLM answers a question" to "LLM operates as an agent": given an objective, it
decides what to do, uses tools to do it, observes results, and loops until done. Building these
reliably is the skill you is learning.

## Key idea I keep coming back to
Accuracy compounds *down* across steps — five steps at 90% each ≈ 59% success. The fix is the
[[wat-framework]]: let the agent **orchestrate** but push deterministic work into reliable tools.
